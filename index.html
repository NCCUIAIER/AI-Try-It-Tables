Table 2 · 5–10 Minutes

🔍 Citation & Credibility

AI can help you find sources — but you verify them. Practice the SIFT method and fact-check an AI response.

Your Progress0%
The SIFT Method
S — Stop
Pause before you share or cite. Is this source trustworthy?
I — Investigate
Look up the author, publication, and date. Who is behind this?
F — Find Better Coverage
Search for other sources that confirm or challenge the claim.
T — Trace Claims
Go back to the original source of data or quotes.
Activity: Fact-Check an AI Response
Ask an AI: "What are 3 recent breakthroughs in AI research?" Then use SIFT to verify one fact.
